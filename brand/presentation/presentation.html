What we heard

The brief

A lightweight Windows desktop reader that instantly reads selected text aloud with natural human speech when pressing Ctrl+Shift+R.

instantacousticunobtrusivecrispmodern

Recognizable at 16 px in Windows system tray, distinct from generic speech bubbles, evokes text selection and voice synthesis, works in monochrome and brand indigo.

The Soundwave Cursor

  • Unifies the two core user actions: selecting text (caret) and listening (soundwaves)

Recommendation & next steps

Our recommendation

The Soundwave Cursor — it uniquely tells the 'select text and speak' story without falling into standard chat bubble tropes, and scales down with zero ambiguity to a 16 px tray icon.

  • Choose a direction (or name what you like in each).
  • We refine it: geometry, optical corrections, lockups.
  • Colour and typography options for the chosen direction.
  • Final artwork, variants and mini guidelines.
